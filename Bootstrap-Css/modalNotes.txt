Modal

A Modal is a popup window that appears on top of the current page without navigating to another page.
The user must interact with it (close it or complete an action) before returning to the main page.


A Bootstrap modal is made of five main parts.

Modal
│
├── modal
│
├── modal-dialog
│
├── modal-content
│
├── modal-header
│
├── modal-body
│
└── modal-footer


Centered Modal
By default, a modal appears near the top of the screen.
A centered modal appears in the middle of the viewport.

Complete Login Modal
A login modal lets users sign in without leaving the current page.
Instead of navigating to a new page, the login form appears as a popup.

Examples:
E-commerce websites
Dashboards
Admin panels
Portfolio websites


Static Backdrop Modal

data-bs-backdrop="static"
This prevents closing the modal by clicking outside.


To also disable the Esc key:
data-bs-keyboard="false"
